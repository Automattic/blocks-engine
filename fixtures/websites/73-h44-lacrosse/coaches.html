Coaching Staff

Led by a Division I athlete, our staff brings real college-level coaching to Jacksonville — one staff per age group, all focused on development and getting players seen.

#44 Jonathan Huber · Founder
Founder & Director

Jonathan Huber

Division I lacrosse player at Stony Brook University and St. John's University, where he wore number 44 — the namesake of H44 — and was known for scoring a ton of goals. Founded the club to bring elite, Long Island–style coaching and a real college-recruiting pipeline to Jacksonville.
